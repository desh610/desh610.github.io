Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how RD Projects ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our mobile applications and games (the "Apps") published on Google Play. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

1. Information We Collect

Our games are designed to be enjoyed without creating an account, and we do not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. Game progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device.

Our Apps use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, primarily to serve ads and keep the Apps running:

  • Advertising identifiers and related data (e.g. Google Advertising ID), used by our ad partner to show relevant ads.
  • Device & usage information such as device model, operating system version, general (non-precise) location derived from IP, and app interaction data.

2. Ads & Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements and Google Play Services for core app functionality. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy — policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information — policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob — AdMob & privacy

3. How Information Is Used

  • To display and measure advertisements.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the Apps.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical issues.

4. In-App Purchases

Some Apps offer optional in-app purchases (e.g. removing ads or buying coins) processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will remove it.

6. Data Retention & Your Choices

Locally-stored game data remains on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the App. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings under Google → Ads.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at rdprojects24@gmail.com.
